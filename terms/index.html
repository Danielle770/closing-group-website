What we do

The Closing Group is a marketing and coordination platform. We bring together people who are each interested in buying a new apartment from the same builder, so that the builder can offer them a better price. Our services are limited to:

  • presenting apartments and information about them;
  • gathering interested buyers and negotiating a group price and terms with the builder, as a licensed real estate broker;
  • introducing buyers to independent professionals, such as Israeli law firms and banks or other finance providers.

That is where our role ends.

  • We do not guarantee any price, saving, financing, timeline or result.
