What we do

We organise groups of buyers who together purchase new apartments that builders have not yet sold, with the aim of reaching a price close to the builder's cost. We act as a licensed real estate broker and coordinator. We are not the seller, not a lender and not a law firm. Each purchase is made directly between the buyer and the builder, under a separate written sale contract, with the buyer's own lawyer.
