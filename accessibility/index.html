Accessibility statement

Last updated: 9 November 2026

The Closing Group believes everyone should be able to find a home and use our website independently, with dignity and with equal access. This statement explains what we have done to make the site accessible, what is still being improved, and how to reach us.

Standard we follow

We have built this website to meet Israeli Standard IS 5568, which is based on the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines WCAG 2.0 at level AA, as required by the Equal Rights for Persons with Disabilities (Service Accessibility Adjustments) Regulations, 2013. We also follow WCAG 2.1 AA where we can.

What we have done

  • Clear page structure with real headings, landmarks and navigation menus that screen readers can announce.
  • Every function works with a keyboard alone, with a visible focus outline showing where you are.
  • Text and background colours meet WCAG AA contrast ratios.
  • Buttons and links are at least 44 × 44 pixels, so they are easy to tap.
  • Every form field has a written label, and form messages are announced to assistive technology.
  • Pages resize and reflow on phones, tablets and computers, and text can be enlarged to 200% with the browser zoom.
  • Animations are gentle and switch off automatically if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Decorative graphics are hidden from screen readers.

On 9 November 2026 the site passed an automated WCAG 2.1 AA check with no errors found. Automated tools cannot catch everything, so we also welcome feedback from users.

Browsers and assistive technology

The site is designed to work with current versions of Chrome, Safari, Edge and Firefox on computers and phones, and with common screen readers such as VoiceOver, NVDA and TalkBack.

Known limitations

  • Some content, such as project details and deal reports, is still being added. New content is checked for accessibility before it is published.
  • Documents from third parties (for example, builders' plans or bank forms) may not be fully accessible. On request, we will provide the information in another accessible format.

Accessibility coordinator

If you have difficulty using any part of this site, find something that is not accessible, or need information in another format, please contact our accessibility coordinator. We will reply as soon as possible.
