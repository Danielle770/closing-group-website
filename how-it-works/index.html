Questions people ask

How much will I save?

Our goal is the builder’s cost, which typically means hundreds of thousands of shekels below the list price. The exact figure is set in negotiation and shown next to real reported deals, so you can judge it yourself.

Who pays the lawyer and the broker?

The builder does, as part of the group deal. You pay no agent fees and no legal fees, and we disclose the arrangement to you in writing.

Can I visit before I commit?

Yes. The apartments are ready, so you or someone you trust can walk through them. We also offer live video tours for buyers abroad.

I live abroad. How does the money get to Israel?

Our bank partner helps you open an account and transfer funds safely. Ask us about locking in your exchange rate.

What taxes apply to me?

Purchase tax depends on your residency and whether this is your only home. Our law firm will explain your exact position before you commit.
