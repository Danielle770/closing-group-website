HOW IT WORKS

A ready apartment. One package. No pressure.

Here is every step, from joining your kahila to holding the keys, and every safeguard that protects you along the way.

Six steps to your keys

01

Join your kahila

Free membership. Verify your email and WhatsApp, and tell us where you are and what you are looking for.

02

Explore ready apartments

See the area, the apartments, the floors and the fair price check built from real reported deals nearby.

03

Reserve your place

Choose your apartment and place a deposit. It goes straight to our partner law firm’s trust account, never to us.

04

We negotiate as one

With a full kahila, we bring the builder enough interested buyers for his remaining stock and negotiate one group price, aiming for his cost.

05

Visit and sign together

Walk through your finished apartment. Each buyer then signs their own sale contract with the builder, on the same signing day, with a lawyer of their choice.

06

Get your keys

We introduce you to a bank that can help with transfers from abroad. The apartment is ready, so you move in or rent it out.

Questions people ask

How much will I save?

Our goal is the builder’s cost, which typically means hundreds of thousands of shekels below the list price. The exact figure is set in negotiation and shown next to real reported deals, so you can judge it yourself.

Who pays the lawyer and the broker?

Not you. The Closing Group is a partnership between a licensed real estate broker and an Israeli law firm, with years of experience in the Israeli property market, built to protect you as the buyer. Developers pay us a fixed, agreed fee for using the platform, because clearing their remaining stock in one large deal frees up major credit lines for them. Our law firm represents buyers only, so it works for you and your interests alone. The full arrangement is disclosed to you in writing before you sign.

Can I visit before I commit?

Yes. The apartments are ready, so you or someone you trust can walk through them. We also offer live video tours for buyers abroad.

I live abroad. How does the money get to Israel?

We introduce you to a bank that can help you open an account and transfer funds. Exchange rates and financing are between you and the bank.

What taxes apply to me?

Purchase tax depends on your residency and whether this is your only home. The partner law firm, or your own lawyer, will explain your exact position before you commit.
