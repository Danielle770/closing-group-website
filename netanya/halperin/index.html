Reading an Israeli listing

"5 rooms" in Israel means 4 bedrooms.

Israel counts the living room as a room. So every apartment here, listed as a 5-room apartment, gives you four bedrooms plus a large living and dining space opening onto the balcony.

How the group works

Nine buyers, one builder, one price.

  1. Join the Netanya Kahila for free. No payment and no commitment.
  2. Choose your apartment. Your ₪50,000 deposit goes into the trust account of our partner law firm, Evyatar Himi, Ir Yamim, Netanya. It never goes to us.
  3. We negotiate one group price with the builder, for everyone.
  4. You sign with our law firm beside you. Abroad? Sign remotely with a power of attorney from the Israeli embassy.
  5. Bank Hapoalim's foreign residents department helps with transfers from abroad.
₪390,000–₪488,000 Our goal: the saving on a regular apartment, about 12–15% below the builder's list price. A goal, not a guarantee. The final price is fixed only in the signed contract. The builder pays our fee. Buyers pay no broker fee.
