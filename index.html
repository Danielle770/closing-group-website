How it works.

Here's exactly how it works — six steps, and we walk you through every one. You decide; we handle the rest.

  1. 1

    Join your Kahila

    Join free. Tell us where you are and what you're looking for — that's it, you're in.

  2. 2

    Explore ready apartments

    See the real apartments — the area, the floors, the fair price check built from real reported deals nearby. No brochures, no drawings: finished homes you can actually visit, or tour live on video.

  3. 3

    Reserve your place

    Choose your apartment and place your deposit. It goes straight into our partner law firm's trust account — never to us. Your place in the group is reserved, and the terms for getting your deposit back are written down before you pay anything.

  4. 4

    We negotiate for you

    We know what the apartment cost the builder — and we still negotiate the best deal possible for the group. One group, one price, down to his cost. You don't negotiate anything; we do it for you.

  5. 5

    Sign, with our law firm beside you

    This is the part everyone worries about, and it's the easiest part — because our law firm is with you the whole way. They check every contract, explain everything in plain English, and they're beside you on signing day. You sign once your money is in Israel. Can't fly in? No problem — you sign remotely, with a power of attorney from the Israeli embassy. One signing day, everyone together.

  6. 6

    Get your keys

    We introduce you to a bank that can help with transfers from abroad. The apartment is ready — you move in, or rent it out.

משרדי מעניק ללקוחות מעטפת משפטית מקיפה לאורך כל שלבי העסקה – החל מבדיקת הנכס והזכויות בו, דרך ניהול המשא ומתן, עריכת הסכם הרכישה, היבטי מיסוי מקרקעין וליווי הליכי המימון, ועד להשלמת העסקה ורישום הזכויות על שם הרוכשים.

המטרה שלי היא להעניק לכל לקוח ביטחון משפטי, שקט נפשי וליווי אישי ומקצועי, תוך שמירה מלאה על האינטרסים שלו לאורך כל הדרך.

My name is Evyatar Himi. I was born and raised in Israel; I'm married and a father of four.

I have been a lawyer since 2008 and a notary, with wide experience in Israeli property and real estate. Over the years I have guided many clients through buying and selling property, including homes, luxury properties, investment properties and urban renewal deals.

One of my main specialties is legal guidance for foreign residents who want to buy property in Israel, whether to invest, to live in, or as part of preparing for future Aliyah.

My office gives clients complete legal cover at every stage of the deal: from checking the property and the rights in it, through the negotiation, the purchase agreement, real-estate taxation and the financing process, to completing the deal and registering the property in the buyers' names.

I believe that buying a property in Israel, especially for those who live abroad, is far more than a financial transaction. For many, it is a connection to the Land of Israel, building a future for the family, and fulfilling a dream.

My goal is to give every client legal certainty, peace of mind and personal, professional guidance, fully protecting their interests the whole way.
