Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

This policy explains what personal information The Closing Group ("we", "us") collects through closinggroupisrael.com, why, and what your rights are. We follow the Israeli Protection of Privacy Law, 5741-1981 and its regulations. For visitors in the United Kingdom and European Union, we also respect the rights described in the GDPR.

Who we are

The Closing Group is operated by Ram Even Haim, a licensed real estate broker in Israel (Ministry of Justice license no. 30928389), currently operating as a sole trader. You can contact us about privacy at any time:

What we collect

When you join a kahila or contact us, we collect the information you give us:

  • Full name, email address and mobile / WhatsApp number
  • Country and city, and optionally your community or congregation
  • Which area (kahila) you are interested in
  • Your consent choices and when you gave them
  • Anything you write to us, and later, the apartments you show interest in

If you use the "Ask us anything" chat to leave a question, we collect your name, your WhatsApp number, your question and the page you asked it on, so our team can get back to you at a time that suits you.

If you reached us through a link from a community leader (a link ending in ?ref= and a name), or you tell us who sent you, we record that name with your sign-up or question so we can credit the leader. Your browser remembers the referral name on this site so it's still there if you sign up later.

When you open an answer on our Questions & Answers page, we count which answer was opened, without linking it to you, to show the most asked questions.

You are not legally required to give us this information, but without your email and phone number we cannot verify your membership or tell you about groups.

When you visit the site, our web server automatically records technical information such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, for security and to keep the site running. We do not currently use advertising or analytics cookies. If we add them, we will update this policy and ask for your consent where required.

Why we use it

  • To create and verify your membership
  • To match you with groups and apartments in the areas you chose
  • To send you alerts about new groups, last apartments and groups about to close, only if you agreed (by WhatsApp and email). You can unsubscribe at any time.
  • To arrange viewings, introductions to our partner law firm and bank, and the purchase itself, if you decide to go ahead
  • To meet our legal obligations as a licensed broker, and to protect our rights

Who we share it with

We never sell your information. We share it only when needed:

  • Our partner law firm, Evyatar Himi (Ir Yamim, Netanya), and our bank, Bank Hapoalim, when you ask to move forward with a purchase
  • The builder, only your name and the apartment you chose, and only once you commit to a group
  • Service providers that host our website and send our emails and WhatsApp messages, under confidentiality and only for these purposes
  • Authorities, where the law requires it

Some of these providers may store information outside Israel. We only use providers that protect personal information to an appropriate standard.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you are a member and for as long as needed for the purposes above or as the law requires. If you leave the kahila, we delete or anonymise your details unless we must keep them by law.

Security

The site uses an encrypted (HTTPS) connection. Access to member information is limited to the people who need it to serve you. No system is completely secure, but we take reasonable measures to protect your information.

Your rights

  • See the information we hold about you
  • Ask us to correct or delete it
  • Withdraw your consent to alerts or marketing at any time
  • If you are in the UK or EU: object to processing, ask for a copy in a portable format, and complain to your local data protection authority

To use any of these rights, contact us using the details above. In Israel you may also contact the Privacy Protection Authority.

Changes to this policy

We will post any changes on this page with a new "last updated" date. Significant changes will also be sent to members by email.
